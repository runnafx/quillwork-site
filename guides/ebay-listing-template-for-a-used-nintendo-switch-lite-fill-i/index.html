eBay Listing Template for a Used Nintendo Switch Lite (Fill-In)

A fill-in eBay listing for a used Nintendo Switch Lite, covering the title, item specifics, condition notes, photo list, shipping and buyer questions. It also has a finished example you can copy and change for your own console.

You've tested the console, reset it and cleaned it. Now you need a listing that shows up in search, answers buyer questions before they're asked and doesn't lead to a "not as described" return. This page gives you every part of that listing as a template with blanks to fill in, and then a finished example.

Before you start, check eBay's sold listings for your exact color and condition. Use the Sold and Used filters and look at console-only listings. Prices change by color and by season, so set your price from what has actually sold recently, not from asking prices.

Part 1: The title (80 characters max)

Buyers search by model, color and condition words. Put the most important words first and skip filler like "WOW" or "L@@K".

Template:

Nintendo Switch Lite [Color] Handheld Console HDH-001 [Tested / Reset] [Extras]

Fill-in rules:

  • Color: use Nintendo's name for it: Yellow, Gray, Turquoise, Coral or Blue. For special editions, use the edition name as printed on the box, for example "Zacian and Zamazenta Edition" or "Hyrule Edition".
  • Tested / Reset: only write "Tested" if you actually ran the full test (power, screen, buttons, stick, cartridge, Wi-Fi, audio, charging).
  • Extras: "w/ Charger", "w/ Case", "w/ Box" or "Console Only". Only list what's actually in the box you ship.
  • For a broken unit, start with "For Parts" and state the fault: "Nintendo Switch Lite Gray For Parts - No Power - HDH-001".

Examples:

  • Nintendo Switch Lite Turquoise Handheld Console HDH-001 Tested Factory Reset
  • Nintendo Switch Lite Coral Console w/ Charger - Tested, Reset, Works Great
  • Nintendo Switch Lite Yellow For Parts Repair - Stick Drift - Powers On

Part 2: Condition setting

eBay gives you a short list of condition options. For a console you've tested, the honest choices are:

  • Used: it works fully and any flaws are cosmetic and described.
  • For parts or not working: any functional fault at all. That includes stick drift, a dead pixel cluster, a cartridge slot that won't read, a battery that won't hold charge, swelling or no power.

If you're unsure which applies, pick "For parts or not working". Calling a faulty console "Used" is the quickest way to end up with a return and negative feedback.

Part 3: Item specifics

Fill in every field you can. Buyers filter by these, and listings with blank specifics can drop out of filtered searches.

  • Brand: Nintendo
  • Model: Nintendo Switch Lite
  • Platform: Nintendo Switch
  • Type: Handheld Console
  • Color: [Nintendo's color name]
  • Storage Capacity: 32 GB
  • Region Code: [check the box or listing source; US units are normally sold as NTSC-U/C or Region Free]
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth
  • Features: [only what's true, e.g. "Touchscreen", "Wi-Fi Capability"]
  • MPN / Model Number: HDH-001
  • Includes: [Console only / Charger / Case / Original Box]

Part 4: The description, as a fill-in block

Keep it short and easy to scan. Put the testing results first, because those are what the buyer is worried about.

Template:

Nintendo Switch Lite, [Color], model HDH-001.

Tested and working:

  • Powers on and charges with [charger used]
  • Screen: [no dead pixels / note any]. Touchscreen responds across the whole screen.
  • All buttons, shoulder buttons and the D-pad work
  • Both analog sticks tested in System Settings stick calibration: [no drift / note any]
  • Reads game cartridges: [tested with any title]
  • Connects to Wi-Fi
  • Speakers and headphone jack work
  • MicroSD slot reads a card

Reset: factory reset to the setup screen. No user account or Nintendo Account is attached. It's ready for your own account.

Cosmetic condition: [describe honestly, e.g. "light scuffs on the back, small scratch on the lower right of the screen, visible only with the screen off. See photos."]

What's included: [list exactly, e.g. "Console only. No charger, no games, no case."]

Please note: the Switch Lite is a handheld-only model. It does not connect to a TV, and its controls are built in (no detachable Joy-Con).

Ships within [1 / 2] business days, packed with padding in a box.

Why the "please note" line matters: some buyers, especially gift buyers, don't know the Lite won't connect to a TV. Saying so up front stops "item not as expected" messages before they start.

Part 5: Photo checklist (aim for 10 or more)

Shoot on a plain, neutral background in daylight or bright, even light. Photograph the actual unit you're selling, never stock images.

  1. Front, screen off, straight on
  2. Front, screen on, showing the Home menu or the setup screen
  3. Back, full view
  4. Close-up of the model label and HDH-001 text. Cover or blur the serial number if you prefer.
  5. Top edge: shoulder buttons, game-card slot, headphone jack
  6. Bottom edge: USB-C port
  7. Each side
  8. Close-up of every scratch, scuff or flaw you mention in the description
  9. The stick calibration screen showing centered sticks (optional, but buyers find it reassuring)
  10. Everything included, laid out together

If a flaw shows up in your photos, mention it in the text too. If you mention it in the text, show it in a photo.

Part 6: Shipping setup

  • Weight: the console alone weighs about 0.6 lb (roughly 275 g). Packed in a small box with padding, it usually comes to around 1 to 1.5 lb. Weigh your actual package; don't guess.
  • Box: use a box, not a padded mailer. A bent mailer can crack the screen.
  • Padding: wrap the console in bubble wrap so it can't move. Then shake the box. If you hear anything shift, add more padding.
  • Service: a tracked service is the sensible choice for electronics in this price range. Whether you offer free shipping or calculated shipping, account for the label cost in your price.
  • Handling time: only promise what you'll actually meet. In November and December, gift buyers often filter by delivery date.

Part 7: Answers to questions buyers ask

Copy these into your messages or add them to the description.

  • "Does it work with a TV?" No. The Switch Lite is handheld-only and has no TV output.
  • "Is it banned or locked?" "It's been factory reset with no account attached. I tested Wi-Fi and it connects." Only add "I confirmed it can reach the eShop" if you actually checked.
  • "Does it have drift?" Quote what you saw in the calibration test. Don't guarantee it for the future.
  • "Can you ship today?" Answer with your real handling time.
  • "Will you take $X?" Decide your lowest acceptable price before you list. If you turn on Best Offer, set an auto-decline floor at that number.

Finished example

Title: Nintendo Switch Lite Turquoise Handheld Console HDH-001 Tested Factory Reset

Condition: Used

Description:

Nintendo Switch Lite, Turquoise, model HDH-001.

Tested and working:

  • Powers on and charges with an official Nintendo adapter
  • Screen has no dead pixels. Touchscreen responds across the whole screen.
  • All buttons, shoulder buttons and the D-pad work
  • Both sticks centered in System Settings calibration, no drift seen
  • Reads game cartridges (tested with a retail cartridge)
  • Connects to Wi-Fi
  • Speakers and headphone jack work
  • MicroSD slot reads a card

Reset: factory reset to the setup screen. No account attached.

Cosmetic condition: light scuffs on the back and one small mark on the top edge near the game-card slot. The screen has no scratches visible with the screen off. See photos 3, 5 and 8.

What's included: console only. No charger, games or case.

Please note: the Switch Lite is handheld-only and does not connect to a TV.

Ships within 1 business day in a padded box with tracking.

Final check before you click "List"

  • Title is under 80 characters and includes "Switch Lite", the color and HDH-001
  • Condition matches your test results
  • Every item specific is filled in
  • Every flaw is in both the text and the photos
  • "Included" lists exactly what's in the box
  • The handheld-only note is in the description
  • Package weighed and shipping cost checked
  • Price set from recent sold listings for your color and condition, not from asking prices
  • The console is signed out of all accounts and shows the setup screen

Olympus Stylus Epic (mju-II) eBay Listing Template: Fill-In

A fill-in eBay title, item specifics, description and photo list for selling an Olympus Stylus Epic / mju-II film camera. Includes a finished example and the wording to avoid so buyers don't return it as "not as described."

The Olympus Stylus Epic, sold as the mju-II in Europe and Japan, is one of the most searched film point-and-shoots on eBay. Buyers who want one know a lot about it. They look at the lens marking, check whether the flash works and ask about scratches on the lens. They also pass over any listing that might be a zoom model.

Follow this template and your listing will answer those questions before anyone asks, show up for both names, and be accurate enough that a return is hard to justify. Copy the brackets and fill them in.

This page assumes you've already confirmed the camera is a real fixed-lens Stylus Epic (the lens ring reads 35mm 1:2.8, with no zoom rocker and no "Zoom" on the body). If you haven't, do that first. Zoom 80, 115 and 170 models sell for much less, and calling one an "Epic" is the fastest way to get a return.

Step 1: The title (80 characters max)

Buyers search for both names, so include both. Put the facts that matter most first.

Template: Olympus Stylus Epic mju-II 35mm f/2.8 Film Camera [DLX/QD if present] [Tested/Film Tested] [Extra]

Rules:

  • Use "Film Tested" only if you shot a roll and saw the developed negatives or scans. If you only checked that it powers on, fires and the flash goes off, write "Tested". If you haven't tested it, write "Untested" and price it that way.
  • Spend leftover characters on things buyers actually filter by: "Case", "Strap", "Black", "Date" (QD/DLX versions).
  • Don't use "rare," "mint" (unless it truly is) or "L@@K." They take up characters and buyers don't trust them.

Step 2: Item specifics

eBay uses item specifics for search filters. Fill in every one that applies and leave blank any you can't confirm.

  • Brand: Olympus
  • Model: Stylus Epic (put mju-II in the title and description too)
  • Type: Point & Shoot
  • Film Format: 35mm
  • Focus Type: Autofocus
  • Color: [Black / Champagne / other]
  • Condition: Used, or For parts or not working if any function fails
  • Country/Region of Manufacture: fill this in only if it's printed on the camera

Read the condition carefully. On eBay, "Used" means the item works as described. If the flash is dead or the lens cover sticks, list it as For parts or not working and say exactly what fails. Buyers looking for a cheap repair candidate shop that category on purpose.

Step 3: The description (fill-in)

Copy this block and keep it short. Most buyers skim the bullet points and then look at the photos.

---

Olympus Stylus Epic / mju-II, 35mm f/2.8 fixed lens, [DLX/QD date version / standard version]

This is the fixed-lens model (35mm 1:2.8 marked on the lens ring), not a Zoom 80/115/170.

What I tested:

  • Powers on with a fresh CR2 battery: [Yes / No]
  • Sliding front cover opens and closes smoothly and turns the camera on and off: [Yes / Sticks / Fails]
  • Lens extends and retracts: [Yes / No]
  • Shutter fires: [Yes / No]
  • Flash fires: [Yes / No]
  • Flash mode button cycles through the modes on the LCD: [Yes / No]
  • Rear LCD: [all segments clear / faded / missing segments]
  • Film door latches closed: [Yes / No]
  • Film test: [Shot a roll of (film type) on (date); negatives/scans shown in photos / Not film tested]

Condition:

  • Lens glass: [clean / light marks (describe) / scratches visible in photo #_]
  • Viewfinder: [clear / dust specks / haze]
  • Body: [describe wear honestly, e.g. "light rubbing on front corners, small scuff near shutter button, see photo #_"]
  • Battery door and film door: [intact / cracked / loose]

Included: [camera / strap / case / original box / manual / battery] Not included: [battery / film / anything shown only for reference]

[Shipping line: how it's packed, e.g. "Ships in a padded box, not a bag."] [Returns line: your actual return policy.]

---

Why "Not included" matters: buyers who see a battery or a roll of film in your photos will expect to receive it. Say so in writing if it isn't part of the sale.

Step 4: Photos (shot list)

eBay allows up to 24 photos. For this camera, the photos sell it more than the text does. In order:

  1. Front, cover closed, on a plain background. This is your main photo.
  2. Front, cover open with the lens extended.
  3. Close-up of the lens ring showing "35mm 1:2.8". This is the most important photo because it proves the model.
  4. Lens glass at an angle under a light so marks or haze show (or show that there are none).
  5. Top, showing the shutter button and the LCD.
  6. LCD lit with the camera on, all segments visible.
  7. Flash firing. Take it in a mirror or with a second phone timed to the shot.
  8. Back with the film door closed, then a photo with it open showing the inside.
  9. Bottom, including the battery door and any DLX/QD or date markings.
  10. Every flaw, each in its own close-up.
  11. Everything included, laid out together.
  12. Film test proof, if you have it: a few scans or the negative strip held up to light.

Skip stock photos and other people's sample shots. They suggest you're hiding the actual item.

Step 5: Price and format

Don't copy a price from this page or from active listings. Asking prices for this camera vary a lot and many listings never sell.

  1. On eBay, search olympus stylus epic and separately olympus mju ii.
  2. Filter to Sold Items and Used.
  3. Throw out zooms, parts units, lots and anything with a different lens marking.
  4. Look at the sold prices for units in the same state as yours (film tested vs tested vs untested, with or without case). Write down the lowest and the typical price.
  5. To sell quickly, list a little below the typical recent sold price. If you can wait, list near the typical price and turn on Best Offer.

Fixed price or auction? For a well-known camera that sells steadily, Buy It Now with Best Offer usually gives you more control than an auction ending at a random hour. Use an auction if you have no comparable sales and want the market to set the price, and set a starting price you'd be okay accepting.

Worked example (filled in)

Title: Olympus Stylus Epic mju-II 35mm f/2.8 Film Camera DLX - Tested + Strap

Description:

Olympus Stylus Epic / mju-II DLX (date version), 35mm f/2.8 fixed lens. This is the fixed-lens model (35mm 1:2.8 on the lens ring), not a Zoom 80/115/170.

What I tested:

  • Powers on with a fresh CR2 battery: Yes
  • Front cover slides smoothly and turns the camera on and off: Yes
  • Lens extends and retracts: Yes
  • Shutter fires: Yes
  • Flash fires: Yes
  • Flash mode button cycles through the modes on the LCD: Yes
  • Rear LCD: all segments clear
  • Film door latches closed: Yes
  • Film test: Not film tested. Electronics and mechanics tested only.

Condition:

  • Lens glass: clean, no scratches seen under a flashlight (photo 4)
  • Viewfinder: a few dust specks, which don't show on film
  • Body: light wear on front edges and a small scuff beside the date panel (photos 10-11)
  • Battery and film doors: intact

Included: camera and wrist strap. Not included: battery (removed for shipping), film, case.

Ships in a padded box, not a bag. 30-day returns accepted.

The listing says "Tested," not "Film Tested," because no roll was shot. The dust is disclosed with a note on whether it affects photos. The scuff has a photo number. A buyer can't credibly say this listing misled them.

Wording to avoid

  • "Works great" with no list of what you tested. It's vague, and buyers read it as untested.
  • "Film tested" when you only dry-fired it.
  • "mju-II" on a Zoom model to catch search traffic. That's a not-as-described return waiting to happen.
  • "No returns" used as a shield. eBay's Money Back Guarantee still covers items that don't match the description, so accuracy protects you better than the policy does.
  • "Perfect for beginners/rare/cult classic". It's filler, so put those characters toward condition details.

Pre-publish checklist

  • Title includes both "Stylus Epic" and "mju-II" plus "35mm f/2.8"
  • Tested / Film Tested / Untested wording matches what you actually did
  • Lens ring photo showing 35mm 1:2.8 is included
  • Every flaw has a photo and a line in the description
  • Condition is "For parts" if anything fails
  • "Not included" line covers the battery, film and any props in the photos
  • Price is based on filtered Sold results for comparable units, not active listings
  • Package is padded and the box is sturdy enough for a camera with a protruding lens cover

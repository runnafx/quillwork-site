Money Clones bot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The Money Clones bot is a private Discord bot used only in Quillwork Studio's own server.

What it reads

It reads messages in that server only to relay the server owner's messages to Quillwork Studio's automated tools. Messages from other users are ignored and not stored.

What it stores

The owner's messages and the replies are stored on the operator's own computer to keep the conversation history. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Removal

The server owner can delete the stored history at any time by removing the bot and asking the operator.

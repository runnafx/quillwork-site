Money Clones bot: Terms of Service

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The Money Clones bot is a private Discord bot run by Quillwork Studio for its own server. It is not offered to the public and cannot be added to other servers.

Use

The bot posts status updates from Quillwork Studio's automated business tools and relays messages from the server owner to those tools. Only the server owner's messages are acted on; messages from anyone else are ignored.

No warranty

The bot is provided as is, without any warranty. Messages it posts may be generated with AI assistance and may contain mistakes.

Changes

These terms may change at any time; the current version is always on this page.
